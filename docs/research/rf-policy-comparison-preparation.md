# Matched-policy numerical preparation

The first actual comparison attempt is closed as an
[explicit range-guard abort](rf-policy-comparison-first-range-stop.md), with zero
RAW records. The frozen numerical contract remains unchanged; RF response has
not been determined.

This preparation freezes supplied-record eligibility and a 75-slot numerical decision for the [matched software-policy proposal](rf-policy-comparison-readiness.md). It is an offline composition of the existing waveform, receive and original RAW AC-statistics libraries. It does not establish the specimen's loaded software arm, acquisition freshness, capture health or permission to execute a transition. The machine-readable numerical contract is [contract.toml](../../experiments/rf-policy-comparison/contract.toml).

The scheduled numerical slots are A1 stock, B derived and A2 stock, each with five repeats at 100, 800, 975 and 1000 MHz followed by a second 100 MHz visit. The initial and final 100 MHz groups remain distinct by visit index. Exactly 75 unique arm/visit/repeat slots are required. Missing, duplicate, unplanned or rejected slots produce an unassessable decision; the helper does not infer a missing arm or replace failed records. Identical waveform hashes are permitted, including legitimate repeated constants. Fresh RUN/STOP and query-sequence evidence belongs to the independent controller and captured management streams.

Eligibility is identical for all arms. The helper reparses the original waveform and both preambles, qualifies RAW acquisition geometry, and uses the public `measure_raw_ac` primitive on the original decoded binary64 volts. The full record must contain 100,000 samples, acquisition count one, start one and stop/memory 100,000. Actual rate and the preamble increment must satisfy the frozen 4 GSa/s/250 ps geometry and relative tolerance `1e-8`. Original samples must be finite, extrema must be strictly inside ±180 mV, and Vpp must be strictly below 360 mV. These hard requirements are not waived for a weak arm.

Every record retains the unchanged default receive receipt. Eligible received records carry its qualification. Weak records may instead carry only a named interpretation: `range:vpp-range` after independent proof of the low branch `0 ≤ Vpp ≤ 5 mV`, or receive-stage `band-peak-frequency`, `global-peak-frequency` or `energy-fraction`. No other receive failure is allowed. In particular, malformed bytes, display mode, geometry/rate problems, overload and `spectrum:non-dc-energy` cannot become weak eligibility. A weak record supplies sampled AC statistics without establishing carrier identity or a physical fundamental amplitude.

The common primary metric is unwindowed demeaned population AC RMS, in original volts. Exact decoded constants retain exact zero AC and their constant DC value. A varying record whose required statistics become unrepresentable is rejected, so arithmetic underflow cannot masquerade as an exact constant. Ordinary decimal-to-binary64 rounding remains part of decoding; constant means equality of decoded samples, not equality of the original decimal spellings. Tone fitting is not part of this eligibility or decision helper.

The decision computes arithmetic means and descriptive population SD for five records in each of the 15 groups. It reports uncorrected values. Ten controls must pass before a primary contrast can be interpreted: A2/A1 mean-RMS return at the initial 100, 800, 975 and 1000 MHz visits, each arm's final/initial 100 MHz return, and pairwise initial 100 MHz means across arms. The inclusive return limit is ±0.3 dB, implemented as the binary64 RMS-ratio interval `[1/(10**(.3/20)), 10**(.3/20)]`. A zero numerator or denominator makes the return ratio unavailable, leaves the finite dB field absent and makes the decision unassessable. Equal zero means are not assigned a fabricated 0 dB return.

At 1 GHz the helper retains all 50 comparisons of the five B records against the ten A1/A2 records. A positive engineering contrast requires every B/A pair to reach the frozen RMS ratio `10**(1/20)`; the mirrored condition produces a contrary result. Finite dB values use a difference of logarithms, avoiding ratio overflow in reporting. Named positive-versus-zero relations retain no invented finite dB value, while both-zero pairs pass neither direction. Unavailable return controls still block the primary conclusion. Intermediate pair contrasts are unresolved. The 800 and 975 MHz pairs are separately retained secondary controls and cannot substitute for the 1 GHz criterion.

The pure models and functions are experiment-specific ignored tooling under `out/rf/policy-comparison-tools/`. `qualify_comparison(ComparisonRequest)` returns immutable eligible/rejected models. `decide_comparison(ComparisonDecisionRequest)` operates on their supplied receipts. The file adapter `comparison_cli.py` accepts explicit arm, visit, repeat, waveform paths and acquisition observations; it emits TOML with `comparison-eligible`/exit 0 or `comparison-rejected`/exit 1. Root evidence hashes bind the original bytes. Nested `receive` and `statistics` tables preserve the public receipt fields and schemas. Claim flags remain false for verified physical arm, carrier identity, calibrated amplitude and final group decision.

Synthetic controls exercise original 100,000-point records and complete scheduled fixtures. They distinguish eligibility from the numeric contrast: low/zero records are eligible in all arms, while an all-zero complete fixture is structurally complete and numerically unassessable. Positive, contrary, intermediate and one-pair failures are separate fixtures. Hard overload/geometry/rate/malformed/underflow controls, the three receive allowlist branches, public receipt equality, missing/duplicate slots and return-control failures are retained for independent review. These are supplied-record controls, not experimental acquisitions or software-transition witnesses.

The numerical helper supplies no correction for the uncalibrated source, folded harmonics, reboot/thermal effects, analog tuple state or DSP selection. A future positive supplied-record contrast requires separate verified arm and transition evidence before a physical association is argued. It cannot establish isolated analog gain or a corrected fundamental transfer function.
