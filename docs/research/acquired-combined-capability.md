The [acquired catalog sequence](acquired-option-catalog.md) accepted ten ordinary
individual licenses cumulatively under stock MHO984 capability. The earlier
[acquired capability comparison](acquired-capability-comparison.md) retained only
FlexA. This experiment asks whether the complete accepted catalog, especially
its three bandwidth options, changes original option policy under the separately
derived MHO984D capability selection.
